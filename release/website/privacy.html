Privacy Policy · Effective November 20, 2026

Your scans stay on your device.

LanScope is provided by Xi'an Zhuimu Technology Co., Ltd. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated service that receives your scan history.

Information stored locally

LanScope stores your scan settings, language preference, and up to 30 recent scans on your device. A scan may include its target, timestamps, responding IP addresses, port results, web page titles, device names, and the Wi-Fi name when available.

We do not upload this information to our servers, sell it, or use it for tracking. You can delete individual records from Scan History. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups and restoration are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings.

Network traffic needed for scanning

When you start a scan, your device sends requests directly to the targets you specify. Local discovery may also use Bonjour, NetBIOS, SSDP/UPnP, and reverse DNS. Domain scans use the system DNS resolver. Web detection requests a limited amount of HTTP/HTTPS content from responding services and does not submit credentials or execute page scripts.

Target servers, DNS providers, and your network operator can see the requests and network information such as your source IP address. Their handling of that traffic is governed by their policies. Public scans may use cellular data. Opening a discovered web link uses your browser and that website's policies.

Permissions

Local Network: used to discover and communicate with devices on your Wi-Fi network.

Location: requested only to obtain the current Wi-Fi name when iOS permits it. LanScope does not request location updates, read geographic coordinates, or store location history. You may deny this permission and continue scanning.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, retain it only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. Ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This support website contains no advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting and email providers process ordinary connection or delivery information to operate their services. Visiting this website does not send us the app's scan history.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's privacy practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact yinzhim@gmail.com.
